### CLIM-000030: Conditions for self-sustaining marine ice-cliff failure
- **Subfield:** Ice sheets, glaciers, and sea ice
- **Open:** Determine when cliff failure can outrun viscous thinning, mélange buttressing, and geometric stabilization. Establish observationally constrained conditions for sustained retreat.
- **Status:** Influential since the 2010s; Morlighem et al. (2024) found stabilizing processes can prevent MICI in modeled twenty-first-century settings.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Tests a proposed contributor to high-end sea-level projections.
- **Key works:** Morlighem et al. (2024), [“The West Antarctic Ice Sheet may not be vulnerable to marine ice cliff instability during the 21st century”](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.ado7794).
- **Related:** Rate-dependent fracture; granular-jamming buttressing.
